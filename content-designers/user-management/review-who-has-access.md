# Review Who Has Access

People join and leave, and partners finish their work. A short review each quarter keeps every account matched to a person who still needs it, which is what security reviews and audits ask for.

## Steps

1. Go to **People** (`/admin/people`) and sort by **Last access**.
2. For each account that has not been used for a while, ask the client whether the person still needs access.
3. Block or cancel the accounts nobody needs. Cancelling disables the account and keeps its content, so the history of who wrote what stays. See [Delete User](delete-user.md).
4. Check partners' and freelancers' accounts against the end dates noted when they were added.
5. Check the roles of the accounts that stay: a person who changed jobs may need a smaller role. See [Edit User](edit-user.md).

## Good Habits

- One person, one account, with a work email address. Never a shared login.
- Partners and freelancers get their own account, the narrowest role that fits, and a noted end date. See [Add User](add-user.md).
- On the day someone leaves, block or cancel the account.
