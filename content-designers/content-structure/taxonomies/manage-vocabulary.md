# Manage Vocabulary

## Manage the Taxonomies

To manage the taxonomy follow these steps:&#x20;

1. Go to **Manage.**
2. Click **Structure** tab.
3. Click **Taxonomy.**
4. A separate page will load with all the list of vocabularies.
