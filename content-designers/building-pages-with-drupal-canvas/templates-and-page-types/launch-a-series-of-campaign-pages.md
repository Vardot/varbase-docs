# Launch a Series of Campaign Pages

When a client runs campaign after campaign, build the first page well and copy it for the next. Every page in the series then has the same shape, and each new one takes minutes.

## Steps

1. Build one campaign page well: a strong hero and one action, benefits, proof, and the same action again. See [Common Page Types](README.md#common-page-types).
2. For the next campaign, open the **Pages** panel in the editor and use **Duplicate page**. Change the words and images.
3. In **Page data**, write a **Meta description** and choose a sharing **Image**; without them, search results and shared links fall back to generic text.
4. Add the page to a menu if visitors should find it there: a new Canvas page does not appear in menus by itself. See [Menu Management](../../url-management/menu-management/).
5. Agree the date the campaign comes down, and who unpublishes it.

## Tips

- Give each campaign a short address for posters and radio. See [Give a Campaign a Short Address](../../url-management/give-a-campaign-a-short-address.md).
- Keep page deletion for senior roles; unpublish a finished campaign instead.
