# Content Designers

This section is the Varbase learning pack for the people who plan, sell and run Varbase 11 sites: project managers, product owners, content designers and editors. It follows the 20-lesson course one to one. Each lesson theme explains what a feature is, why it matters, how to use it step by step on a real Varbase Starter site, and ends with a **Check Yourself** box.

## How Each Page Works

- **What it is**: the feature in plain words.
- **Why it matters**: the outcome it buys a client or a team.
- **Scenarios**: tested step-by-step paths, as the course shows them on the studio site.
- **Questions clients ask** and **Planning notes**: honest answers and where effort really goes. Estimates are labelled as assumptions.
- **Check yourself**: the questions from the end of each lesson.

The screenshots come from one demo site built for the course with **Varbase Starter**: English, Arabic and French content, the default roles, and the optional AI recipes applied. Your site may show fewer items if it does not use those recipes.

## Varbase 11 Highlights for Editors

- **Drupal Canvas** for building pages visually, with ready-made patterns.
- The **Gin** administration theme and the **Navigation** sidebar, with a **Dashboard** to start from.
- **CKEditor 5** with an AI assistant, spell checking and media.
- An editorial **workflow** with review, scheduling, content locking and autosave.
- A shared **Media Library** with focal points and AI alt text suggestions.
- Built-in **accessibility checks**, **SEO analysis** and **cookie consent**.
- A **Trash** that lets you restore deleted content.
