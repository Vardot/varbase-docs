# Content Designers

This guide is for the people who plan and run a Varbase 11 site day to day: content editors, content and site administrators, SEO and marketing staff, project managers and product owners. It covers the editorial features that come with Varbase, from writing a blog post to building a landing page and keeping the site findable, accessible and secure. Each section explains what a feature is, why it matters, and how to use it step by step, with screenshots from a Varbase Starter site.

## Varbase 11 Highlights for Editors

- **Drupal Canvas** for building pages visually, with ready-made patterns.
- The **Gin** administration theme and the **Navigation** sidebar, with a **Dashboard** to start from.
- **CKEditor 5** with an AI assistant, spell checking and media.
- An editorial **workflow** with review, scheduling, content locking and autosave.
- A shared **Media Library** with focal points and AI alt text suggestions.
- Built-in **accessibility checks**, **SEO analysis** and **cookie consent**.
- A **Trash** that lets you restore deleted content.
