# Varbase: Content Designers Course

A free video course on Varbase 11 for project managers, product owners and content designers. Lessons of about 15 to 20 minutes each follow a Varbase Starter demo site in English, Arabic and French, from what Varbase is to building pages, the design system, AI and planning a client project. New lessons are added here as they are published. Each lesson page lists its chapters and the documentation sections that go deeper.
