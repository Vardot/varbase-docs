# Lesson 18: AI in Varbase, Part 1

Explain how Varbase delivers AI as optional recipes connected to a chosen provider, demonstrate the AI help editors use every day (writing assistant, alternative text, image descriptions and tags, tag suggestions, search engine fields), and explain the controls around it (brand context, guardrails, logging, consent and cost).
