# Lesson 16: Accessibility

Explain what web accessibility means and why it matters to clients, use the built-in Varbase checks (the Editoria11y checker, its report and the dashboard alerts), recognise the editorial habits that prevent most problems, and plan accessibility work and testing in a project.
