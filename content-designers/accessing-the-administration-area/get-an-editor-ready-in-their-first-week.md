# Get an Editor Ready in Their First Week

If an editor can do these five things, they are ready for daily work. Use the list as the last exercise of their training, on their own account.

## The Checklist

1. Log in, and check which environment you are on. See [Website Environments](website-environments.md).
2. Find your drafts in **My Drafts** on the dashboard. See [Dashboard](dashboard/).
3. Open **Coffee** with **Alt + D** and jump to the content listing. See [Coffee](coffee.md).
4. Filter the listing to your own content type and language. See [Find Content Fast](find-content-fast.md).
5. Open one of your posts on the site, then edit it from its tabs.

## Planning Notes

- Email delivery must work on each environment, or password reset links never arrive.
- On multilingual sites, decide whether each person chooses their admin language on their profile, or the language follows the address (the default).
- Hand over these docs with the training: the Content Designers section is free to share with a client's editors.
