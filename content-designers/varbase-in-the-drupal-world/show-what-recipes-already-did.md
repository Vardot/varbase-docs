# Show What Recipes Already Did

Recipes are tested setup steps that are applied once. On any Varbase site you can show a client their effect in a few clicks: features that nobody on the project had to choose or configure.

## Steps

1. Go to `/admin/config/content/honeypot`: **Honeypot**, from the Drupal CMS anti-spam recipe, already protects every form with a hidden field that spam robots fill in.
2. Go to `/admin/config/user-interface/antibot`: **Antibot**, from the Varbase security recipe, checks that a real browser sends each form.
3. On the **Dashboard**, the **Top tasks** block and **Choose recommended add-ons** (the **Project Browser**, a catalogue of extra features from the Drupal community) come from Drupal CMS. So do the accessibility checks, the privacy consent manager and the basic search engine set-up.
4. Go to `/admin/config/ai`: the **Providers** group is the one connection to AI that every AI feature on the site uses, also from Drupal CMS.

Nobody on the project had to choose these modules or decide their settings. See [How Varbase Is Built](../how-varbase-is-built/) for recipes in detail, and [Find Out What an Existing Site Was Built From](../how-varbase-is-built/find-out-what-an-existing-site-was-built-from.md) to see which recipes a site has.
