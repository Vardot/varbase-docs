# Varbase in the Community

Talks, podcasts and interviews about **Varbase** from across the Drupal community. Each entry credits and links to its original source.

## Podcasts

### Talking Drupal #461: Distributions

{% embed url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6PKcwFN2IXM" %}
Talking Drupal #461: Distributions (July 29, 2024)
{% endembed %}

Rajab Natshah and Mohammed Razem of Vardot join the Talking Drupal hosts to explain what a Drupal distribution is, its benefits and drawbacks, and how **Varbase** works as a starter kit for public-facing websites. The episode also looks at how recipes and the Starshot initiative, now **Drupal CMS**, shape the future of distributions.

Source: [Talking Drupal, episode 461](https://talkingdrupal.com/461).
